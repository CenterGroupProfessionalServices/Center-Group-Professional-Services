Pressure washing relies on high-pressure water streams to blast away tough dirt on hard surfaces like concrete driveways. Soft washing uses low pressure combined with specialized eco-friendly cleaning detergents to eliminate algae and mold from sensitive surfaces like roof tiles, stucco, and painted walls without inflicting high-pressure damage.
